PLANE
Support and privacy

PLANE is a camera app that previews perspective correction before you take a photo.

Support

Camera and saving

Allow camera access. Correction previews are free. Saving requires an active annual saving plan and permission to add photos to your photo library.

Purchases, restoration and cancellation

Choose Restore purchases in the purchase screen or camera settings, using the Apple Account used for the purchase. The annual saving plan renews automatically. Manage renewal and cancellation in your Apple Account subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. Check the App Store price at purchase.

Silent mode

Silent mode is a one-time add-on usable while your saving subscription is active. It saves from the live feed, so its resolution differs from normal capture. Restoring silent mode alone does not enable saving or silent capture without an active saving subscription.

Privacy policy

Scope

This policy applies to the PLANE iOS app.

Camera, motion and depth

Camera frames, device pose, detected planes and depth data on supported devices are processed on your device for capture and correction. The app has no feature that sends this information to developer-operated servers.

Photos and metadata

With your permission, captured images are added to your photo library. RAW is also saved when supported by the capture configuration and settings. JPEG metadata includes available capture date, exposure and lens information, plus device pose, camera parameters and correction settings. Sharing a photo may also share its metadata, depending on the sharing method. iCloud Photos synchronization follows your device and Apple settings.

Settings, advertising and tracking

Language and camera settings are stored locally. There are no developer accounts, advertising services, analytics SDKs or advertising-identifier tracking.

Purchases and restoration

Apple’s App Store processes purchases and payments. PLANE uses purchase information verified by StoreKit to determine saving and silent-mode access. The developer does not receive payment-card details. Apple’s policies apply to its processing.

Managing data

Deleting the app normally removes its local settings. Photos saved in your photo library, backups and purchase history may remain. Manage images in Photos and subscriptions in your Apple Account.

Contact and external pages

If you email support, your email address and message content are sent to the support contact. Visiting this support site or external pages such as Apple’s is also subject to the relevant hosting or service provider’s policies.
